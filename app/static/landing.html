Get started in 3 steps

No downloads, no installs — just one command

1

Sign up and get your token

Create an account in the dashboard and generate an access token. Each token gets its own permanent tunnel URL.

2

Run one SSH command

Open your terminal and paste the command. Replace PORT with your local service port.

ssh -p 2222 -R0:127.0.0.1:8080 your-token@ssh.iraglobaltech.com
3

Share your URL

Get a public HTTPS URL instantly. Share it with anyone — they can access your local service from anywhere.
